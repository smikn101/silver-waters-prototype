Jill Kelly, spiritual consultant

Grounded guidance with intuitive awareness

Jill has spent decades studying healing and awareness practices, and she pairs them with many years of coaching. Her approach starts with clarity, then adds intuitive tools when they will help.

She went deep into dowsing, including three years of intensive study with a dowsing master, and has worked with a rod and pendulum for many years to clear both spaces and people. She is a Reiki Master and trained energy healer, and has read tarot and oracle cards for over 40 years.

A Buddhist practitioner for most of her adult life, Jill teaches mindfulness meditation and has attended and coordinated more than 60 workshops and retreats.
